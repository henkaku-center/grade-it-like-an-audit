# Lab 3 — Penguin measurements (handout)

Analyze the class penguin dataset (`penguins.csv`, 344 rows, 3 species) and submit a short
report.

Requirements:

1. **Clean the data.** Rows with missing values may simply be dropped — listwise deletion
   is acceptable for this lab; just say what you removed.
2. **Analyze.** Compare flipper length across species with summary statistics and one
   statistical test of your choice. Report the test you used and why.
3. **Figures.** Include at least two figures relevant to your analysis.
4. **Write-up.** Two pages maximum: methods, results, one limitation.

Submit `report.md` (plus any code) in your folder. You may use the course AI assistant;
its transcript is submitted automatically alongside your work.
