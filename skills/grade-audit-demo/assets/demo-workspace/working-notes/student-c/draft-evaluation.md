**Snapshot.** A tidy non-parametric analysis with an unusually honest limitation. As C puts
it: "Gentoo penguins stand apart most sharply, matching what the boxplot shows at a
glance."

**By component.**

  remainder stated.

  reported without degrees of freedom. How to avoid: report the test statistic with its
  df.*

  thoughtful addition that makes the island–species confounding visible.
- **Write-up clarity: 5/5** — clear methods and results; the limitation candidly concedes
  the test was a safe default rather than a justified choice, which is exactly what the
  section is for.

**Total: 18/20.**

**Feedback (subject-facing draft).** What went well: the island figure and the candid
limitation show real analytical self-awareness. What to improve: report H with its degrees
of freedom.
